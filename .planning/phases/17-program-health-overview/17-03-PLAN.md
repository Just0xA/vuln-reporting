(WeasyPrint PDF / openpyxl Excel / inline-CSS email — no JS, no browser). No
`high`-severity threat introduced — all `mitigate`. The PII risk class (T-17-05) is
the established locked QUAL-05 pattern, re-covered here at the render boundary with an
explicit test.
