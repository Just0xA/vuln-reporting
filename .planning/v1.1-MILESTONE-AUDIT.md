- `privacy_label = group.get("privacy_label", "Confidential")` + `scope_subtitle = _format_scope_subtitle(...)` resolved once per group
- `_CHROME_AWARE_SLUGS` gate at `run_all.py:725` injects `privacy_label`, `scope_subtitle`, `report_title` only for chrome-aware slugs
- `board_summary.run_report()` (`reports/board_summary.py:108,269-275`) constructs `PdfChromeConfig` and passes `pdf_chrome=` to `ReportComposer` (line 284)
