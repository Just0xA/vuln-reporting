owner's accumulated backlog of overdue, unfixed vulnerabilities and renders a
per-owner RAG rating across the four standard channels (PDF, Excel, email panel,
analyst detail) plus the cover-page RAG strip.

High findings** per owner. No debt-dimension classifier (missing-patch / legacy /
misconfig) — explicitly out of scope, future work.

backlog" cut of the same data, using the modular composition pattern (no new

`composed_report` `modules:` in `delivery_config.yaml`).
