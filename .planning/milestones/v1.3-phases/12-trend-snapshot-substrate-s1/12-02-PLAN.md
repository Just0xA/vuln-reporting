<objective>
Deliver the forward-accumulating snapshot engine `data/trend_store.py` with two functions — `capture_snapshot(...)` (writes an atomic monthly snapshot of open counts) and `read_trend(...)` (cold-start-safe month-over-month reader) — plus the content-test suite proving atomicity, per-month idempotency, cold-start safety, PII discipline, and zero regression to the existing `management_summary` trend store.

The engine is **df-injected** (D-08): it receives already-fetched `df` (open+reopened findings) and `assets_df`, calls `open_findings_at` (Plan 01) to compute the open subset, groups by `severity`, records the in-scope `asset_count` (D-04), and writes atomically to a substrate-owned `trend_{dimension}_{tagsuffix}.json` file. It NEVER touches `management_summary.py`'s private helpers or its `management_summary_*.json` files (D-01, D-05) — that structural separation is what guarantees TREND-03's byte-for-byte bar.

Purpose: The persistence + read API that every v1.4 trend report consumes; forward-compatible with Phase 13 owner iteration via the `dimension` + `tag_filter` parameters.
Output: `data/trend_store.py` and `tests/content/test_trend_store.py` (marked `pytest.mark.content`).
</objective>
