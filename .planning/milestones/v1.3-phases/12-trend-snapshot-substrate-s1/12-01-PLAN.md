<objective>
Deliver the canonical, pure-compute open-count primitive `open_findings_at(df, date)` in `utils/` and its labelled unit-test suite. This is the keystone of the entire trend substrate: the snapshot engine (Plan 02) and entry point (Plan 03) both depend on it being exactly correct.

The predicate implements the **reopened-aware two-interval model** proven in Spike 002. The naive single-interval form (`first_found <= D AND (last_fixed IS NULL OR last_fixed > D)`) is a bug — it silently drops the entire REOPENED population (~19% / ~30,500 of 160,453 findings in the spiked dataset). The two-interval model corrects this exactly (+2 of 160,453).

Purpose: A single, unit-tested call site for "which findings were open at point-in-time D", reusable by every v1.4 trend report.
Output: `utils/open_count.py` (pure, no I/O, UTC-aware) and `tests/unit/test_open_count.py` (OPEN/REOPENED/FIXED labelled cases, marked `pytest.mark.unit`).
</objective>
