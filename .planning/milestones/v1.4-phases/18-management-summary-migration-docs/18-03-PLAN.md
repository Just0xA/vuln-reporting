<objective>
Reconstruct ~12 months of real MoM history (2025-06 → now) for the ALL-ASSETS
scope (severity/all_assets) from Tenable's now-retrievable fixed+open exports and
seed the S1 trend store BEFORE the cutover (D-18-01, D-18-08). This is its OWN plan
that runs and is verified independently — the migration (Plan 04) then simply reads
a store that already has history. The whole sequencing design (D-18-10) keeps a
real build out of the 2,200-line atomic removal.

SCOPE DECISION (review HIGH change #4 — explicit, not an unspoken gap): reconstruction
seeds the ALL-ASSETS history ONLY. Confirmed against delivery_config.yaml: NO active
delivery group runs management_summary (only a commented example), and tag-scoped
trend cold-start is the pre-existing behavior for board_summary / composed_report.
Building reconstruct-per-tag-scope is therefore OUT of scope. Tag-scoped delivery
groups cold-start their MoM history — acceptable, pre-existing, NOT a regression.
Plan 05's runbook documents this; Plan 04 reconciles it with the no-regression
success criterion (no active group is tag-scoped on management_summary, so nothing
regresses).

The reconstruction is paired with audit-honesty guardrails that MUST NOT be
simplified away (CONTEXT specifics): provenance-marked immutable snapshots
(D-18-03), faithful partial backfill with null asset_count rather than a
fabricated denominator (D-18-04), a partial flag on taper-edge months (D-18-02),
and an embedded overlap-test against ground truth (D-18-09) that gates the whole
write. DOC-02 trust is the point.

CURRENT-MONTH IMMUTABILITY (review MEDIUM change #7 — pinned): reconstructed months
— INCLUDING the current month — are NEVER overwritten by a later capture_snapshot()
run. capture_snapshot() must skip any month that already has a source='reconstructed'
entry, so the first migrated run (Plan 04) cannot clobber a reconstructed current
month. This is the rule consistent with D-18-03.

MONTH-BOUNDARY SEMANTICS (review MEDIUM change #8 — pinned): all month-boundary math
goes through ONE shared helper month_end_utc(month), tested at exactly 00:00:00 and
23:59:59, with inclusive/exclusive bounds and tz settled (month key local per CLAUDE.md
timezone policy; generated_at UTC; boundary comparison UTC).

Purpose: Ship migrated trend with 12mo of REAL all-assets history rather than
cold-starting, with provenance honesty an auditor can trust.
Output: idempotent all-assets seeding script + month_end_utc helper + reconstructed-
month immutability in capture_snapshot + overlap-test gate + unit tests. Operator
runs the script once (checkpoint) to seed before cutover.
</objective>
